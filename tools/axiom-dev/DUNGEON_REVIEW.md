# Dungeon and magic review

Open the Dungeon and magic section of the Dev Panel in a solo playtest.
Enter Spectator mode to jump to a room, complex, or Manyfold viewpoint.
Use Inspect crosshair to read the selected block's collision and mirage flags,
any concealed mimic in reach, and the player's local up vector.
Preview impact plays an existing spell effect without changing blocks or mana.

In survival, crouch and interact with a passable mirage, or hit it with
Lumen Drill. The reveal writes a bounded group of loaded illusion blocks to
the world. A concealed mimic presents the ordinary Open Chest interaction.

Unopened dungeon caches receive seeded potions and arcane materials.
Survey caches in a dungeon also hold a fine wand if a wand record is available.
Already recorded caches keep their saved contents.
Review extra side-room chest locations in a disposable benchmark world with
`--benchmark-generator-version=251` and the normal benchmark options.
This generator preview includes cumulative generation changes and is separate
from ordinary world creation.

In the Hall, use solid cover or dismantle the Chain Astronomer's StarGlass ring.
Water reduces the Furnace Saint's heat attacks and creates a longer recovery.
Both guardians pay additional crafting materials through their defeat record.
