# World tone: structures, districts and routes

Date: 2026-11-06. Owner direction for generated and authored places. Canon is `WORLD_LORE.md`; history layering is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-civilization-on-solved-geography-design.md`.

Owner words: quiet beauty, solitude, responsibility, possibility, panacea or curse. Players can be nomadic without fear.

## Rules for a place

1. **Every structure has a job in the anchor's working life.** It moved, held, weighed, watered or recorded something. No job, no structure.
2. **Infrastructure continues past the district edge.** Rail, road, pipe or stair reaches another place, or stops at a cause the player can find.
3. **Departure was orderly.** Doors shut, goods stacked, machines stood down. Damage came later and has a physical cause.
4. **Wear follows use.** Worn treads, patches and added rooms sit where people walked and needed space.
5. **Scale shows the loss.** Platforms, halls and stairs are sized for more people than the world now holds. Counterweights sit above the marks worn for them.
6. **Buried structure shows only where the ground is cut.** River banks, cliffs and slopes expose the anchor; terrain stays geological.
7. **A restorable machine's cost is visible.** A pump's water comes from a basin the player can see; a lit district draws on a source that dims. No verdict on whether it was worth it.

## The braid sets the clock

Every three world days a carriage descends; its light runs down the filament and the districts facing it brighten in turn. This is the one moving thing every place shares, and the first thing a new player should see.

## Nomadic without fear

- Canon already honours the traveller: those going up left a spark at the toll for those staying down. Passing through is part of the anchor's life, not a failure to settle.
- A place left alone keeps its state while the player is away.
- Shelter sits along old routes, where travellers once needed it. Depends on routes between districts existing.

## Unverified

- Whether the braid is visible from the Hills spawn.

## Rejected

- Look, palette, figures or scenes from reference games. This includes a neon accent on dusty ground, a lone figure before a dead giant, a protagonist sick with the world's sickness, murals as the default storytelling.
- Frozen last moments; they contradict orderly departure.
- Terrain shaped as buried streets and rooflines.
- Setpieces chosen first and explained afterwards.
- Mechanics invented to carry mood: debts that follow the player, base defence, decay timers.
- New proper nouns, factions or lore without owner approval.
