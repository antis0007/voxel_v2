# Reference brief: 200 Downloads images (2026-10-23 set)

Source: every image saved to `%USERPROFILE%\Downloads` on 2026-10-23 between 20:45
and 20:53 (200 files), read in four batches. Colours are sRGB estimates by eye.
The set is painterly (Hubert Robert, Panini, Piranesi, Romantic oils, modern
concept art). It holds no pixel-fluid (Noita) references; that look comes from
the request, not the images.

## Rules that hold across the whole set

1. Warm light, cool shadow. Outdoor shadow is blue-violet (#5A6AA8 to #3A4A78)
   at 35-45% of lit value, never neutral grey or black. Underground shadow is
   warm umber (#2A1A10 to #2B2A24).
2. Strong, coloured aerial perspective. Far forms lose 50-80% contrast and take
   the sky colour: cool (#A8C8D8, #8FA8D8) away from the sun, gold or pink toward
   it. A 1-3 m mist band lies on water and in valleys.
3. Quiet masses, dense detail only at edges, frames, bases and light/shadow
   boundaries. Big wall, floor and vault areas stay flat.
4. Overgrowth follows faces: moss and weeds on up-facing tops, vines hang from
   wall tops and arch undersides, rubble and undergrowth gather at wall and
   column bases.
5. Small saturated accents (flowers, roofs, fire) on greyed masses. No
   posterization: gradients stay smooth.

## Water

- Depth absorption shifts hue: shallows jade/turquoise #3FC9B0-#5FB59A, 1-3 m
  #1E8F86, deep teal-navy #135A6E to #0E3550. Red falls off first, green last.
- The bed stays readable through 2-4 m in clear water (rocks, steps, pads).
- Still water is mostly reflection at grazing angles: dark own colour (#22282A),
  reflections 15-25% darker than the source, smeared vertically, broken by
  horizontal ripple bands. Light sources give a broken vertical glitter column.
- Foam is white (#F2F4F0) lace only where water meets rock, shore or a moving
  object; the open surface has none. Thin edges transmit mint (#7FE3C2).
- Falls: milky white strands with blue-grey #9FB4BE shadow, mist at the base
  2-4x the fall width.
- Caustics: thin bright wobbly lines on shallow beds, about 1.3x bed value.

## Fire, smoke, gas, glow

- Fire: core #FFE9A0, body #FFB23A to #F07A1A. Light spill (#C8701E) is local,
  3-5 m, lit faces 6-10x unlit; everything else stays cool and dark.
- Lantern/torch: small hot core, wide soft halo 3-6x the core, warm floor pool.
- Smoke: puffy clustered masses, lit on top (#D8D4CC) and darker underneath,
  never flat grey. Stylised gas palette: lit #FF9A4A, halftone #F05A5A,
  ambient #6A6ADF, occlusion #3A3A9A.
- Magic glow: cream core #FFF2D8, warm bloom #E88A5A, dithered falloff over
  10-20x the core; mint haze #B8E8C8 with gold motes #E0B040 around magic trees.

## Powders, soil, rubble

- Sand lit #F2DDB0 / #C89040, shadow cool lavender #A8A8C8 (0.65x, hue-shifted).
- Dirt paths ochre #B08848 lit, #6A4A28 shade, edges broken by tufts.
- Rubble: angular 0.3-1.5 m blocks with chipped edges and a dark crevice per
  block, densest at wall and column bases.
- Boulders: 2-3 flat planes, moss (#7AA040) on top faces only.

## Trees and foliage

- Giant trees: trunk 1/6 to 1/3 of crown width, root flare 1.5-1.8x trunk
  diameter, 2-6 limbs splitting low (1/3 height), tapering about 50% per fork,
  leaning and twisting 20-40 degrees. Bark grey-olive #6A6650-#9A9478 with moss
  #A8B860 on lit and up faces.
- Crowns are 15-20 clumped lobes with sky gaps, not one blob. Each lobe has
  three tones: lit top yellow-green #D8D040 / #A8D050, mid #5A8A2A, underside
  sky-tinted blue-grey #6A8090 (not dark green). Backlit edges glow gold #F0D060.
- Cypress/conifer: narrow vertical flames 1:6-1:8, dark #1E2A1E, as value anchors.
- Undergrowth dense at bases; vines as dark curtains on the shaded side.
- Fantasy accents: pale glowing trunks, autumn orange #E8A030 canopies, motes.

## Dungeons, temples, ruins, caves

- Value range: 90% of the frame under 10% luminance; one aperture (oculus,
  broken roof, door) at 10-20x the surroundings (#FFF6C8), hard-edged floor
  patch at about 85%, soft dusty god rays at +10-20%.
- Scale: doors 5-8x human height, colonnades of 10-20 repeated columns fading
  30-40% toward fog, vault figures at 1/20-1/40 of height.
- Materials: warm brick, travertine, marble; lit stone cream #E8D6A8, shadow
  warm brown #6A5038 indoors. Coffered vaults #2A2218-#4A3C2C.
- Detail at column bases, capitals, niches and frames; vaults flat.
- Caves: ochre limestone #B08A5A lit / #4A3828 deep, pale bluish exit, moss on
  boulder tops, grey-green pools.
