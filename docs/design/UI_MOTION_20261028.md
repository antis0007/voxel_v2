# UI interaction and material rules

## Hierarchy

- Labels and hit targets stay fixed during hover, press and selection.
- Recess, panel, edge and accent come from the selected palette.
- Selection uses an opaque tinted fill and a persistent indicator.
- Keyboard focus has the same emphasis as pointer hover.
- Disabled controls have no interactive halo.

## Motion

Shared implementation: `egui_ui/motion.rs`.

- Hover: approximately 95 ms in, 135 ms out to 95% completion.
- Press: approximately 55 ms in, 110 ms release.
- Selection: approximately 100 ms in, 125 ms out.
- Exponential interpolation is interruptible from its current value.
- First appearance preserves an existing selection without an entrance animation.
- Underlines grow from the centre. Text, icons and layout do not translate or scale.
- Reduced motion snaps all interaction states immediately.
- Settled controls stop requesting animation frames.

## Materials

- Prism palettes use their own accent and edge hues for rim lighting.
- Two analytic Gaussian halos soften a derivative-antialiased rim.
- Illumination is restricted to an eight-point edge band. Text is never blurred.
- No idle sweep on ordinary panels or controls. Animated spectrum remains confined
  to the existing selected-item treatment and freezes under reduced motion.
- Inventory sprite colours and gameplay lighting retain their separate roles.

## Layout and review

- Short title windows reserve the action stack before adding top spacing.
- Scrollbar space is excluded from button width.
- Existing font roles, UI scale and palette IDs retain their saved meaning.
- Review native GPU exports at compact and desktop sizes, plus interaction and
  reduced-motion captures. Compilation alone does not establish visual acceptance.
