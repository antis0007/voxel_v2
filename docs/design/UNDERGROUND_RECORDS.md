# Underground records and routes

Generator 88 expands buried complexes into cloister, divided-ward and staggered-workshop footprints. The existing cell boundary, roof cover, ramp grade and sealed-objective constraints remain authoritative. Up to three feasible return loops favor the longest tree detours; the first is open, while other service cuts may need excavation. Existing saves retain their generator.

Room sequences follow each ruin's former work: sluices and cisterns; furnaces and foundries; wards and cells; ossuaries and shrines. Side branches hold stores, gardens, crypts or equipment. A reachable ordinary branch becomes a scriptorium, with an intact record pedestal off its central aisle. Small terrain-clipped complexes may have no ordinary branch available.

## Discoveries

| Record | Physical trigger | Permanent benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lamplighter's tally | Read the scriptorium pedestal | Light Shot learned; mana cost -3 |
| Banked ember | Read the porter's record beside a buried gate | Ember Seed learned; active ward reduces fire/lava contact damage by 25% |
| Traveller's countermark | Complete a gate return to Hills | Mirror Ward duration +2 seconds |
| Counterweight rule | Dock the Manyfold weight and open the passage | Gravity learned; mana cost -1 |

Each discovery awards XP once (25, or 40 for returning). Spell cards retain their existing wand requirements. Mana benefits use the shared casting/card calculation, ward duration uses the shared rule, and protection applies only while a ward still has time and charges. It combines multiplicatively with existing contact protection.

Reading requires a live player, a first-hit bookcase within 4.5 m, and its authored pedestal coordinates. Mining the record removes its interaction; reading a different bookcase gives no reward. Lore and benefits can be revisited in the inscriptions panel. Interaction uses the existing F binding and host authority path.

Ownership and XP persist together in progression sidecar VXPRG002; VXPRG001 still loads with no new records. The actor carries both through realm travel. Return credit is granted only after the destination checkpoint and link publication succeed, followed by a new save request. Death does not erase record ownership. Old Manyfold saves whose weight was already docked can recover the discovery by inspecting the socket.

After the Workshop heart and lamp discovery, the compass points to a buried gate. In the Nether it points to a return gate. Manyfold remains distinct from the Nether: displaced inhabited infrastructure is not proof of an afterlife.
