# Workshop co-op

2–4 players. Build a shared workshop, clear the nearby mine, and automate iron.

## Join the same world

1. Extract the zip. Everyone uses the same package.
2. One player opens **Host.cmd**, enters a name, and shares the session code.
3. Friends open **Join.cmd**, enter their names, and enter that code.

Internet access is required. Steam login is not required for the relay package.
For slower computers, use **Host-LowSpec.cmd** or **Join-LowSpec.cmd**.

## First session

Inventory → **Projects** opens Expeditions, Machinery, Tools / Weapons, Wands
and Traversal. Ingredient links open recipes; Uses shows the next production steps.
Part Builder shows costs for the current voxel size. The opening compass marker
ends at the workshop. Use **Track** for another project and **Untrack** to clear
it. **Local Leads** keeps the workshop and directions recorded from residents.
Pin a lead to put it on the map.

- Follow the compass to the workshop. Interact with its bench to set respawn
  when no hostile creature is nearby.
- Gather food and materials. Open the inventory for recipes; prepare a wand or
  melee weapon. Share supplies through dropped stacks and storage.
- Descend the lit mine together, defeat its guardians, then return to the bench.
- Build a generator, cables, electric furnace, item pipe and storage. Fuel the
  generator, smelt iron, and route an iron ingot into storage through the pipe.

The Workbench tab tracks both shared goals. Split the jobs or stay together.
Pipe deliveries appear in storage's Machine tab. After both goals, keep building
and exploring in the same world.

For the next trip, wire a Cultivator to power and connect its item output to
storage. Stand near it and open **Production → Request Fuel**. A batch of
four bio-ethanol uses eight biomass and one fibre. **Shared Stock** shows
what has arrived at each outpost; take the fuel from storage. The finished
request links to the burner jetpack recipe.

## Travel gear

Craft slime boots and a slime sling in the inventory. Hold use on the ground,
aim and release the sling; boots rebound on landing. Hold Sneak to settle.
Craft a hang glider at the workbench from three fibre, three planks and one
copper ingot. Equip its chest slot, then press Jump while falling. Aim down to
dive or up to flare. Press Jump again to fold it.

Later, burner jetpacks use bio-ethanol and induction packs use charged portable
batteries. Hold Jump for thrust; Alt+Sneak toggles hover. These replace the glider
in the chest slot. Slime boots work with either.

## Controls

WASD move · Space jump · Ctrl sprint · Shift sneak · G interact

E/Tab inventory · LMB mine/melee · RMB place/use food · R cast · T next spell · M map

Options shows the active bindings.

## Keep progress

Close through the game menu. Reuse the same extracted folder and player name.
The host keeps the shared world; each joining player keeps their local identity.
Death uses survival rules: recover dropped equipment or ask a friend for help.

Share the original zip, before anyone plays. Keep the `data` folder private and
back it up before moving to a newer package. Extract updates separately.

This is a playtest. The new build still needs a complete party playthrough.
Build details and remaining checks are in BUILD-NOTES.md.
