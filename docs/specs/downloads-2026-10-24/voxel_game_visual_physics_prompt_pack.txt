EVIDENCE AND ART DIRECTION

I inspected image(1).png, the annotated game view; image(2).png, the Noita reference; image(3).png and image(4).png, the game's water views; and image(5).png, the small Noita UI reference. Image 5 is a 38x18-pixel crop of two slots, not a complete inventory-screen reference. The red labels in image 1 are annotations. The center blocks may be a material test arrangement. The partly visible right HUD might be cropped by the capture; a layout defect needs confirmation in a full frame.

The main visual problems are inconsistent shape and material design, excessive small-scale texture contrast, weak separation of lighting values, and water that reads as luminous translucent slabs. Existing shade is visible under the roof and on trunks. These pictures do not establish that shadows, AO, depth-aware water or other effects are absent, only that the final image is not communicating their intended result strongly enough. They cannot establish frame pacing, physics correctness, fluid transport, temporal stability or a particular shader bug.

Priority observations:

1. WATER: Images 3 and 4 show mint-colored vertical slabs, a repeated bright horizontal band, rectangular bright outlines and oversized pale loops on the surface. At close range, the loops dominate the view. Their intended role is uncertain: foam, caustics and reflection require different spatial behavior. The top and side treatments look disconnected. Check actual material IDs and state before deciding what those blocks represent.
2. TERRAIN: Grass has dense lime mottling across almost every visible region. Pale yellow-green soil sides weaken the distinction between plant cover and earth. Stone repeats conspicuous scratch/cross motifs. More resolution alone will not supply material structure.
3. BUILDINGS: The roof is a dense field of narrow regular bands. Wood, roof covering and the masonry in the water views need clearer construction logic, differentiated scale and more restrained repetition.
4. TREES: Image 1 mixes rounded faceted crowns with dense cubic crowns. Image 3 also shows very flat left-side crowns on long thin trunks. This may involve generation, LOD or both. The goal is intentional species structure and coherent style, not simply more leaf blocks.
5. LIGHT: Bright ground and foliage compete at similar values. Open sun, canopy shade, eaves and contact points need stronger but readable separation. Preserve daylight; do not reproduce Noita's near-black areas everywhere.
6. CLOUDS: The marked cloud has an abrupt straight-looking right edge, also visible in image 3. It is a reproducible visual symptom, not proof of a texture, ray-march or history bug.
7. HUD: Image 1 spreads related information across an oversized MP bar, detached MP number, ability icons, separate keycaps, faint spell label, survival rows and inventory strip. The broad compass adds weight at the top. Group information by player task and verify fit in uncropped frames.
8. WAND: Preserve the warm palette, deliberate chunky silhouette, recognizable construction and magical focus. It is a useful quality reference for the rest of the world. Refine its light and flame without replacing its identity.

Proposed direction: an enchanted, tactile voxel wilderness with an ornate gold-and-silver grimoire interface. Keep deliberate block and pixel shapes, use structured low-noise materials, give vegetation strong silhouettes and porous crowns, and reserve vivid emission for spells, fire and discoveries. Water and atmosphere may be smooth phenomena while remaining consistent with the world palette. Use warm wood and highlights, cooler readable shade, varied natural greens and restrained magical accents. The interface uses dark leather, metal inlay, bookbinding corners, silver clasps and small purposeful mechanisms. Concentrate elaborate ornament on expanded book/inventory panels while retaining crisp compact combat controls. This is an original direction, not an instruction to copy another game's assets.

The supplied Noita frame succeeds through quiet background masses, stronger terrain edges, localized firelight and colored action accents. Its material marks explain rock, moss and timber. Transfer that attention hierarchy and the visible relationship between physical events and effects. Its 2D darkness and exact HUD layout are not universal prescriptions for first-person 3D.

Complementary's official comparison shows convincing depth, readable submerged terrain, coherent reflections and foliage that remains recognizably blocky. Its Reimagined and Unbound styles are different default settings, with Reimagined preserving Minecraft's characteristic visual elements and Unbound leaning toward realism [S1]. Photon documents a broad set of lighting, atmosphere, material and temporal effects [S2]. Treat these as references to evaluate, not a checklist to implement wholesale.

PROMPT 2 — REPAIR THE WATER SEEN IN IMAGES 3 AND 4

Apply the shared contract. Make the pictured liquid read as coherent water, with credible depth and flow cues, while preserving actual fluid quantities and terrain boundaries.

Never subtract nonlinear depth values as if they were world-space distances. Diagnose causes before retuning parameters.

Remove internal or duplicate faces where appropriate and make adjacent exposed surfaces agree across cells and chunk boundaries. Derive visible heights and exposed boundaries from actual fluid state. Distinguish still free surfaces, exposed liquid sides and falling sheets according to that state. Do not fill nonexistent cells or smooth the surface through terrain. Keep voxel-built shorelines; avoid accidental cube seams within a connected body of water.

Use one coherent optical treatment: restrained normals, view-dependent reflection/transmission, thickness-dependent absorption and readable shallow-bed visibility. Use consistent distance units for attenuation, for example T = exp(-absorption * distance), with physically sensible nonnegative parameters [S6]. Treat screen-space depth differences as an approximation; provide a stable fallback for invalid, foreground, off-screen, vertical-face and underwater cases. Avoid bright default side tint and repeated edge highlights masquerading as volume. Prevent refraction from pulling foreground geometry into water. Start with a reliable environment-reflection fallback; advanced reflections remain optional and measured.

Determine what the large looping pattern represents. If caustics, place the effect on appropriate submerged receiving surfaces and restrict it by depth/illumination; do not print huge bright loops over the free surface [S7]. If foam, localize it to supported contact, falling-water, compression or agitation evidence and let it decay. Do not outline every cell. If the necessary simulation signal is absent, implement an honest restrained approximation and document it.

Keep decorative waves separate from transported volume. Use world-consistent coordinates and available flow data for directional motion. Match top/side treatment, handle depth/blend order deliberately, and invalidate relevant temporal history after edits. The result must remain attractive with bloom and caustics disabled.

Acceptance: matched images of the supplied stepped installation, a still pool and a shallow bank, plus a short orbit/flow clip around an obstacle. Graphics superiority is a multidimensional target: coherent art, material response, lighting, water, vegetation, visibility, temporal stability and performance. Fix exact reference scenes and settings for comparison. A different sunset, camera or resource pack is not a controlled measurement. Artistic preference remains a human judgment; technical artifact and timing checks complement it.

Noita's official FAQ describes material-based liquids, gases, sand, rigid bodies and simplified chemistry/electricity/thermodynamics [S8]. Its developer talk specifically addresses continuous-world scaling and destructible rigid bodies [S9]. The meaningful target is broader dependable interactions and playable consequences in 3D, not simply more particles.

For illustration, 256x256 is 65,536 cells; 256x256x256 is 16,777,216. Adding a dimension multiplies this particular dense grid by 256 before extra state, neighbours or rendering. This is arithmetic, not a benchmark of either engine. Sparse active work, explicit transfer ownership and deliberate detail levels are therefore architectural requirements to evaluate.

After the workshop proves the integration, expand by one coupled interaction at a time: buoyant/flow-blocking debris; heat conduction and phase changes; wet conductive paths; support failure redirecting liquid; material growth and magic that alters actual collision/resource state. Select the next interaction for its gameplay consequence and measured cost. Keep one material/world truth while allowing specialized solvers and render representations.
