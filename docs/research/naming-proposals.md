# Working-title proposals

Date: 2026-10-24. **Underkingdom remains the placeholder.** These are five unselected creative alternatives for the human's naming decision under GAME_BIBLE B.6. No title-availability claim is made.

| Alternative | What it communicates | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Strata of the Work** | Layered civilizations and the single discipline behind technology and magic. | Distinctive premise, but "the Work" needs explanation before it means anything to a new player. |
| **The Buried Engine** | Restorable infrastructure and the promise of a deeper physical mystery. | Clear exploration hook; gives the technological dialect more prominence than magic. |
| **Echoes Below** | Chorister resonance, surviving histories and an invitation to descend. | Easy to say and atmospheric; communicates little about building or learning rules. |
| **The World's Grammar** | Matter has learnable rules; glyphs, programs and machines share semantics. | Expresses the thesis directly; sounds more abstract than a survival adventure. |
| **The Last Stratum** | Deep time, successive civilizations and the question of what lies beneath. | Strong destination and mystery; may imply a final layer or ending more fixed than the generated world supports. |

## What we adopt

Evaluate names against the fixed premise: descent, material consequence, rediscovered knowledge and two dialects of one discipline. The title may suggest C-Z1/C-S4 history or C-S1/C-S3 learning without promising a particular core reveal.

## What we reject

Do not select a product name implicitly through filenames, UI text or generated lore. Do not name the core's nature or promise a kingdom-management loop merely because the placeholder contains "kingdom."

## What we improve

Use these tradeoffs for the explicit human naming decision after the master plan is reviewable. Preserve Underkingdom consistently until that decision. Availability and audience comprehension remain separate future checks; these proposals establish neither.
