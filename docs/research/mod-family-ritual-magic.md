# Celestial and ritual magic

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Astral Sorcery, Eidolon, Occultism. Claims: [C-N3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets), [C-Z1/3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction), [C-MC4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Astral Sorcery](https://github.com/HellFirePvP/AstralSorcery) and [Eidolon](https://github.com/elucent/eidolon) are author repositories; detailed constellation, attunement and chant mechanics were not traced. [Occultism's author repository](https://github.com/klikli-dev/occultism) explicitly describes rituals summoning entities from another realm to perform magic. Exact storage/miner/servant behavior remains version-specific.

Mapping proposal: multiblock conditions query sky/time/material/plane state; spawned servants use shared utility AI, storage and controllers. M5 rituals, M6 lore/sites; content after the shared rule services exist.

## What we adopt

Place/time-sensitive rituals, discoverable apparatus and workers with an understandable magical role.

## What we reject

Fixed-coordinate secrets without clues, bespoke invisible inventories and assuming a theme establishes a consistent physical cosmology.

## What we improve

Generate clues from actual ritual predicates, allow multiple solutions to gates and make the civilization's religious apparatus perform an observable function. Celestial conditions and hint discoverability remain acceptance targets.
