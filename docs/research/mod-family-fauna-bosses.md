# Fauna, bosses and elite creatures

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Alex's Mobs, Ice and Fire, Mowzie's Mobs, L_Ender's Cataclysm, Mutant Beasts, Rats, Cave Dweller, Grimoire of Gaia, Infernal Mobs, Champions. Claims: [C-MC2/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-F1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl), [C-N3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Alex's Mobs](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/AlexsMobs), [Ice and Fire](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/Ice_and_Fire), [Mutant Beasts](https://github.com/Chumbanotz/MutantBeasts) and [Rats](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/Rats) identify creature references. [Champions](https://github.com/TheIllusiveC4/Champions) explicitly documents configurable ranks/affixes with visible threat indicators. Author pages for [Cataclysm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lendercataclysm), [Mowzie's](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mowzies-mobs), [Grimoire](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/grimoire-of-gaia) and [Infernal Mobs](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/atomicstrykers-infernal-mobs) were reached. Cave Dweller's intended original/variant identity and exact AI remain unverified; other detailed combat behaviors were not traced.

Mapping proposal: utility-AI content, body/projectile controllers, genome/modifier rules and environment-triggered phases; M2 foundation, M6 encounters. Behavior budgets and feel require later runtime work.

## What we adopt

Distinctive readable behavior, creature roles and announced elite abilities.

## What we reject

Stat inflation as the only difficulty, opaque unavoidable attacks and unique physics exceptions for each boss.

## What we improve

Drive phases from readable arena conditions, let breath interact with shared gas/fire and make crew react to hazards. This research does not establish combat quality or performance.
