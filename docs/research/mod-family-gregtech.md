# GregTech CEu

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), GregTech CEu; reactor overlap A.8. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [GTCEu Modern's electric-machine docs](https://gregtechceu.github.io/GregTech-Modern/1.20.1/Gameplay/Electricity/Machines/) describe recipe voltage, energy hatches, casing constraints, optional batch processing, automatic hatch I/O and overvoltage explosions. Their overclock rule trades time against total energy; it is gameplay balance, not real electrical law. [The project](https://github.com/GregTechCEu/GregTech-Modern) identifies the Modern variant; do not conflate it with every GregTech generation.

Reference checklist: material forms, processing depth, voltage tiers, cable loss, overclocking, casings, controllers, energy/input/output hatches, cleanrooms, chemical routes and batching. Material-form/cleanroom details were not individually traced.

Mapping proposal: shared materials/reactions, electric tiers and heat loss, machine layouts and modifiers; M4. Recipe tables are data only once these services exist.

## What we adopt

Processing alternatives with byproducts, meaningful infrastructure tiers and batching that preserves visible outcomes.

## What we reject

Recipe depth as mandatory grind, duplicated machine families with identical purposes and undocumented version mixing.

## What we improve

Explain each extra process through recovery, purity or new capability; convert losses to measurable heat and make overvoltage repairable. A.15's shared reaction engine must cover every dialect.
