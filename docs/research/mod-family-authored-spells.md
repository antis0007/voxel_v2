# Authored and composable spell systems

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Ars Nouveau, Ars Magica, Electroblob's Wizardry; also **Mana and Artifice**, required by original [Part II](../specs/ENGINE_CORE_PROMPT_v2.md#part-ii--required-research-do-before-planning-cite-in-plan) but omitted from the addendum. Claims: [C-N4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets), [C-MC4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Ars Nouveau's guide](https://ars.guide/book/getting_started/spell_casting/) presents spells, glyphs, spellcrafting and Source machinery. [Wizardry's author README](https://github.com/Electroblob77/Wizardry) explicitly favors finding books and casting spells over complex constructs or perk trees. [Mithion's repository](https://github.com/Mithion/Mana-And-Artifice) identifies Mana and Artifice/Ars Magica lineage; exact modern mechanics and original Ars Magica composition were not independently traced.

Mapping proposal: one spell grammar over shared effect primitives, modifiers, projectile controllers and knowledge state; M5, code plus content. Meaningful combinatorial variety needs a behavioral metric, not just counting glyph permutations.

## What we adopt

Understandable spell construction, discoverable ready-made spells and separate progression routes for experimentation and exploration.

## What we reject

Conflating the Ars projects, hardcoded exceptions for each spell and claiming million-fold meaningful variety from syntax alone.

## What we improve

Preview costs and effect ordering, teach composition through experiments, and give spells and technical tools the same world interaction rules. Composition breadth remains an unproved claim.
