# Blood Magic

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Blood Magic. Claims: [C-MC1/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-N3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [maintainer's altar documentation](https://github.com/WayofTime/BloodMagic/wiki/Blood-altars) describes Life Essence, tiered altar runes, item transformation, blood orbs and comparator feedback; [ritual documentation](https://github.com/WayofTime/BloodMagic/wiki/Rituals) describes Soul Network costs, activation, configured areas and Will modifiers. These are mod-version references, not a commitment to their exact values.

Reference checklist: altar tiers, rune upgrades, Life Essence storage, ritual patterns, activation crystals, Soul Network, sigils, Demon Will, area tuning, alchemy, ritual growth and ritual utility. Detailed sigil/Will balance needs further source work.

Mapping proposal: ritual multiblocks, budgeted FieldEmitter, shared modifiers, mana/reagent accounting and creature state; M5 with exotic ritual content M7.

## What we adopt

Power with explicit cost, visibly constructed rituals and configurable areas with feedback.

## What we reject

A blood-specific second energy engine, opaque hidden buffers and mandatory repetitive health depletion as progression.

## What we improve

Offer disclosed cost routes and physical failure consequences; show missing conditions and recurring drain before activation. Ritual effects must act through the same rules as technical devices; balance and legibility remain unproved.
