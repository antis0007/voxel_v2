# Obscuria Collection

Scope: [Addendum A.1](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a1-obscuria-collection-aquamirae-pandorica-lost-souls-obscure-tooltips-loot-journal-fragmentum). Claims: [C-MC3/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-Z2/3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [author's collection](https://obscurialithium.github.io/) describes Aquamirae's frozen ocean, Ice Maze, Ship Graveyard and story, animated Obscure Tooltips, and Loot Journal pickup notifications. This does **not** verify a persistent discovery journal, pressure simulation, puzzle logic or boss-phase implementation. Pandorica, Lost Souls and Fragmentum remain unverified references.

Mapping proposal: themed worldgen/structure templates, arena conditions reading planes, material-derived tooltip schema and a persistent codex; data plus UI work, M3 tooltips and M6 sites. Ocean pressure and puzzle inputs need their owning SDDs; visual atmosphere alone does not establish playable physical puzzles.

## What we adopt

Recognizable environments, local story clues, readable stat effects and compact pickup feedback.

## What we reject

Treating an animated pickup notifier as proof of a full codex; copying names, art or authored encounters.

## What we improve

Make wrecks functional places with cargo, pressure and light clues; disclose tool-stat causes and provide multiple physical solutions to relic gates. These are acceptance targets, not achieved results.
