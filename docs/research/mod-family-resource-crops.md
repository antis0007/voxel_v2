# Resource crops and compact cultivation

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Mystical Agriculture, Botany Pots. Claims: [C-MC1/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Mystical Agriculture's author repository](https://github.com/BlakeBr0/MysticalAgriculture) identifies resource crops, tools and armor; [Botany Pots](https://github.com/Darkhax-Minecraft/BotanyPots) identifies compact pots for growing plants. Exact crop tiers, growth/harvest recipes and hopper behavior were not traced.

Mapping proposal: common growth rules consume moisture, nutrients, light and, for exotic outputs, explicitly priced mana/reagents. Pot layouts are content over the same growth service; M3 farming and M5 magical resource crops.

## What we adopt

Gardening as an alternative automation route and compact, readable cultivation stations.

## What we reject

Free metal generation disconnected from feedstock/energy and a separate growth simulator inside every pot.

## What we improve

Expose input deficits and expected output rate, connect magical crops to the shared conversion ledger, and keep soil/water/heat meaningful. Equivalent progression and conservation remain proposed acceptance requirements.
