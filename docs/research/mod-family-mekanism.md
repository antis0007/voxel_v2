# Mekanism family

Scope: [Addendum A.13](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a13-mekanism--generators-additions-tools): Mekanism, Generators, Additions, Tools. Claims: [C-MC1/2/3/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [author repository](https://github.com/mekanism/Mekanism) documents upgrade tiers, factories, energy cubes, hydrogen jetpacks and ore multiplication. The [official ore-processing reference](https://wiki.aidancbrady.com/wiki/Ore_Processing) provides the enrichment/purification/injection/dissolution chain and chemical-machine inventory. Exact quantities depend on Minecraft/mod version; QIO, SPS and suit-module limits were not individually traced.

Mapping proposal: chemical recipes consume conserved gas/liquid/material inventories; mining uses shared region jobs, suits shared material/modifier rules, QIO shared storage/link services, packing shared serialization, robit shared controllers. M4–M5; fluid chemistry and entity packing are dependencies, not free reuse.

## What we adopt

Increasingly productive processing that demands more infrastructure, useful configuration, modular equipment and helper robots.

## What we reject

Unconditional fivefold matter creation, chemistry disconnected from world matter, and a second nuclear/storage/teleport engine.

## What we improve

Explain gains as recovery from richer feedstock or explicit reagents; account for tailings, heat and dangerous spills. Preserve machine ownership/inventory when packing and reject unsafe partial serialization. Those rules still need acceptance proof.
