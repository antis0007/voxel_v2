# Botania

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Botania. Claims: [C-MC1/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-Z3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [author's Lexicon](https://botaniamod.net/lexicon.html) describes generating/functional flowers, spreader buffers and directed bursts, range-related mana loss, pool storage, binding, item infusion, redstone control, spark transfer and progression entries. These mechanisms form a spatial automation reference; they do not establish thermodynamic conservation.

Reference checklist: resource-consuming flowers, functional flowers, spreaders, pools, lenses, sparks, runic altar, infusions, elven exchange, Gaia encounter, automation feedback and in-game documentation. Recipe quantities and encounter tuning were not independently played.

Mapping proposal: shared reaction/machine tables, mana transport/field queries, modifiers and boss utility sets; M5. The common energy conversion model must price the magical effects before content is called balanced.

## What we adopt

Magic that asks for spatial engineering, local readable transfer and a book close to the world interaction.

## What we reject

A parallel Botania-only simulation, free resource conversion or treating magical terminology as evidence of real physics.

## What we improve

Make light/growth/heat inputs and losses observable, give feedback when a route or binding fails, and let magical automation solve the same gates as machinery. Conservation and onboarding remain acceptance work.
