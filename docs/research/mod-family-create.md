# Create

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Create. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-F1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Create's author README](https://github.com/Creators-of-Create/Create) emphasizes animated components assembled in many arrangements and Ponder's visual in-game documentation. Exact rotational stress equations, train routing and current component lists were not source-traced in this pass; earlier matrix attribution to train addons does not establish base-mod behavior.

Reference study checklist: kinetic sources, shafts, cogs, gearboxes, stress, belts, press, mixer, deployer, moving contraptions, trains and Ponder. These are separate matrix candidates, not claims that twelve mechanisms were fully verified.

Mapping proposal: kinetic graph, shared machine recipes, item transport, structural bodies, rail controller/reservations and Primer demonstrations. Kinetics/contraptions are new systems; content follows, M4 with bodies as a dependency.

## What we adopt

Machinery whose motion explains its purpose, flexible spatial assembly and short contextual demonstrations.

## What we reject

Copying a stress abstraction as a physically validated torque model or representing assembled contraptions as exceptions to body persistence.

## What we improve

Give kinetic failures visible load, support and heat consequences; show the stopped component and cause. Large moving factories and train operations remain measurable performance/design targets.
