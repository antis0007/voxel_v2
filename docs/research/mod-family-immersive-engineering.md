# Immersive Engineering

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Immersive Engineering. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [author's project description](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-engineering) documents hanging wires/connectors, a rotating-wheel multiblock crusher, bucketwheel excavator, waterwheels, windmills, biodiesel, and the Engineer's Manual. It specifies Forge Energy, not a demonstrated AC circuit solver. The [author changelog](https://github.com/BluSunrize/ImmersiveEngineering/blob/1.21.1/changelog.md) includes wire-obstruction behavior.

Reference study checklist: wire spans, connectors, voltage equipment, multiblock formation, crusher, excavator, arc furnace, conveyors, waterwheel, windmill, biodiesel, manual, revolver/railgun. Exact recipes/weapon behavior remain unverified.

Mapping proposal: one electric graph with material wires, multiblock templates, shared recipes/bodies/projectiles and contextual guides. M4; wire collision/lifecycle and excavation require code beyond data.

## What we adopt

Legible industrial scale, machinery with visible process, suspended wires and an accessible manual.

## What we reject

The old matrix's unsupported “AC-style” label and implying bucketwheel output proves finite terrain extraction.

## What we improve

Budget actual excavation, cable heat and structural failure; make missing multiblock parts and blocked outputs obvious. Energy loss and physical resource conservation are our targets, not source-mod facts.
