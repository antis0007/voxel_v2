# Forestry and genetics

Scope: [Addendum A.11](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a11-forestry--binnies-gendustry-magic-bees-career-bees): Forestry, Binnie's, Gendustry, Magic Bees, Career Bees. Claims: [C-MC4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Forestry's author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forestry) documents automatic farms, bee/tree breeding, butterflies, backpacks, mail and energy, and links Binnie's, Magic Bees and Gendustry as addons. Their exact allele/mutation rules, and Career Bees, were not separately verified.

Mapping proposal: shared A.15 genetics evaluator with plane-dependent conditions; growth and mob/scent behavior provide ecology, multiblocks provide farming and reactions provide fermentation. M3 basic farming, M5 genetics; genome persistence/evaluation is new work rather than assumed data-only content.

## What we adopt

Breeding with legible inherited tradeoffs, useful farm supply chains, analyzers and environmental conditions.

## What we reject

Separate genetics implementations per species and opaque random breeding whose conditions require an external wiki.

## What we improve

Make greenhouse conditions follow heat/moisture/light behavior, expose parental traits and why a mutation can occur, and teach inheritance with a nearby experiment. Detailed genetics and pollination performance remain unproved.
