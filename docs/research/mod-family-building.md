# Building variants and useful decoration

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Chisel, Framed Blocks, Carpenter's Blocks, Macaw's furniture/doors/bridges, Supplementaries, Quark. Claims: [C-MC2/3/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-Z2](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Chisel](https://github.com/Chisel-Team/Chisel) describes decorative block variants; author repositories identify [Framed Blocks](https://github.com/XFactHD/FramedBlocks) and [Carpenter's Blocks](https://github.com/Mineshopper/carpentersblocks). [Macaw's Furniture author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/macaws-furniture) was reached; Doors/Bridges were not separately checked. [Supplementaries](https://github.com/MehVahdJukaar/Supplementaries) explicitly emphasizes functional, complementary additions; [Quark](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Quark) identifies small coherent extensions. Exact shapes, mechanisms and connected-texture rules were not traced.

Mapping proposal: shape/skin variants share material properties, collision/support definitions and interaction schemas; objects use existing inventories/signals where applicable. M3 building and M7 polish; meshing/collision variants require code.

## What we adopt

Material-consistent forms, useful everyday objects and cohesive building palettes.

## What we reject

Decoration that silently changes material physics, copied assets and unique machinery implementations hidden in decorative blocks.

## What we improve

Let bridges carry load, doors seal gas when designed to, and furniture convey a place's function/history. Visual, collision and structural quality remain review targets.
