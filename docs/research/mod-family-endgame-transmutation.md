# Endgame sinks and transmutation

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Avaritia, Environmental Tech, ProjectE/EMC. Claims: [C-MC1/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S2](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Avaritia's author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/avaritia) explicitly frames prolonged endgame progression. [Environmental Tech's author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/environmental-tech) lists large multiblocks and void miners; it notes power features differ by version. [ProjectE's author repository](https://github.com/sinkillerj/ProjectE) describes transmutation tables, collectors, condensers and utility equipment. Exact EMC values and replication mechanics were not traced.

Mapping proposal: compiler templates, elemental feedstock ledgers, energy/mana-gated reactions and orbital resource jobs; M7. Information/energy accounting and exploit-resistant conversion are new systems, not another recipe table.

## What we adopt

Ambitious long-term infrastructure goals, stored designs and deliberate material conversion.

## What we reject

Grind as the only endgame, value cycles that create free resources, and literal ore-from-nothing extraction.

## What we improve

Make complex designs and Feed logistics the late-game constraint; map void-miner appeal to finite remote/orbital resources. Closed-loop losses and template information cost remain unproved design requirements.
