# Farming, cooking and kitchens

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Farmer's Delight, Pam's HarvestCraft, Cooking for Blockheads. Claims: [C-MC4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Farmer's Delight](https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight) describes varied meals, soil improvement, scavenging tools and kitchen utilities. [Pam's author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pams-harvestcraft) explains encouraging dietary variety through food balance. [Cooking for Blockheads](https://github.com/TwelveIterations/CookingForBlockheads) documents a recipe book and multiblock kitchen showing recipes available from held ingredients. Exact crop/food counts and nutritional effects are unverified and intentionally omitted.

Mapping proposal: recipe/material tables, shared cooking heat/time/vessel rules, growth conditions and inventory-backed recipe queries; M3. Kitchen indexing depends on the common storage service.

## What we adopt

Useful food variety, tactile preparation stations and a kitchen that explains what can be cooked now.

## What we reject

Thousands of interchangeable filler foods, duplicated recipe evaluators and external recipe memorization as onboarding.

## What we improve

Expose readiness, temperature and missing ingredients, make cooking failures physical, and teach a short seed-to-meal loop. Nutritional and crop diversity should create choices; its balance remains unproved.
