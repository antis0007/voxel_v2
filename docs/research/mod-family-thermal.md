# Thermal Series and powered equipment

Scope: [Addendum A.9](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a9-thermal-series-expansion-dynamics-foundation-innovation-cultivation-locomotion-integration-and-related-redstone-arsenal-simply-jetpacks): Expansion, Dynamics, Foundation, Innovation, Cultivation, Locomotion, Integration, Redstone Arsenal, Simply Jetpacks. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [CoFH documentation](https://teamcofh.com/docs/) separates historical versions; its [1.12 augments page](https://teamcofh.com/docs/1.12/thermal-expansion/augments/) documents machine/dynamo upgrades, stacking and mutually exclusive specializations, including steam conversion and speed/energy tradeoffs. Locomotion and Simply Jetpacks behavior were not separately verified. Historical RF values are not universal.

Mapping proposal: processing recipes, typed ducts, growth/material reactions and shared Modifier/LinkNetwork evaluators; equipment uses shared body thrust/material properties. M4; machinery/modifiers must exist before assigning data-only cost.

## What we adopt

Explicit speed/efficiency choices, approachable machine families, visible face I/O and modular specialization.

## What we reject

Multiple independent ore-doubling or wireless-transfer engines and assuming every Thermal-era feature exists in every release.

## What we improve

Represent generator losses as heat, spilled fluid grenades as actual matter, and cultivation as water/nutrient/light consumption. Augment UI should show net throughput and operating costs before purchase.
