# Factorio-style factory progression

Scope: [Addendum A.10](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a10-factorio-in-minecraft-factorio-features-to-bring-across-wholesale), including Space Age. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-F1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [official science-pack documentation](https://wiki.factorio.com/Science_pack) describes physical lab inputs consumed for research. Broader engine lessons are in [factorio-updates.md](factorio-updates.md); this note does not reverify every train, platform, quality or pollution rule in A.10.

Mapping proposal: research data, shared belt/inserter networks, rail bodies with block reservations, blueprint jobs and drones, signal VM front ends, event-backed production UI. Pollution-as-reacting-gas and exposure-driven fauna evolution are **our proposals**, not descriptions of Factorio. M4 factory core, M6 ecology/defense, M7 orbital logistics.

## What we adopt

Production goals that create logistical problems, readable bottlenecks, reusable construction plans and distinct transport tradeoffs.

## What we reject

Importing every feature wholesale before checking dependencies, infinite physical buffers or conflating measured throughput with a design target.

## What we improve

Connect factories to matter hazards, finite reservoirs and repair; teach the first resource automation through the Primer. Large factories, train derailment and environmental adaptation require separate cost/behavior proof.
