# Colonies, villages and trade

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): MineColonies, Millénaire. Claims: [C-F1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl), [C-Z1/2](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction), [C-S4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [MineColonies' official site](https://minecolonies.com/) describes worker roles, buildings, colony permissions, food supply and defenses. [Millénaire's official site](https://www.millenaire.org/) describes culturally distinct villages, daily jobs, trade, construction and population growth. No source establishes our proposed utility-AI performance, generated languages or coherent deep-time histories.

Mapping proposal: shared mob jobs/needs, reservation and inventory systems, structure jobs, faction state and event histories; M2 foundations and M6 settlements. Culture templates are data after those services exist.

## What we adopt

Settlements with visible work, supply needs, local identities and useful relationships with the player.

## What we reject

Copying real-world cultures as interchangeable lore skins, unbounded worker simulation and scripted omniscience about hazards.

## What we improve

Derive construction, scarcity and incident responses from shared world state; archive knowledge in physical places that survive catastrophes. Civilization coherence and crew responses remain explicit design/acceptance claims.
