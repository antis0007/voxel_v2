# Thaumcraft 4 and 6

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Thaumcraft. Claims: [C-N1/3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets), [C-S1/3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy), [C-MC4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Azanor's project page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumcraft) describes extracting Essentia from objects and an in-world Thaumonomicon; [the author's API repository](https://github.com/Azanor/thaumcraft-api) is the primary implementation reference. The page mixes historical instructions with TC6 downloads. Node-based TC4 and chunk-aura TC6 mechanics must be researched separately; exact research, flux and taint algorithms remain unverified here.

Reference checklist: aspects, scanning, research, book progression, aura/nodes, essentia, alchemy, infusion, instability, flux/taint, golems and foci. This checklist records needed coverage rather than claiming all details verified.

Mapping proposal: material aspect tags, shared reactions/planes, knowledge events, condition rules, utility mobs and VM-backed glyphs; M5–M6.

## What we adopt

Learning by observation, magical industry, coherent apparatus and dangerous byproducts.

## What we reject

Conflating TC4/6, copying research minigames as proof of learning, or bypassing shared machinery with aspect-specific exceptions.

## What we improve

Tie discovered knowledge to performed experiments, make contamination tangible and cleanable, and supply seed-derived hint chains. These proposals require their own model and acceptance evidence.
