# Draconic Evolution

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include), Draconic Evolution. Claims: [C-MC1/2/3/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S2](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: the [author's project page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/draconic-evolution) documents high-tier tools/armor, multiblock energy storage, teleportation, mob farming, player detection and time/weather controls. [The source language data](https://github.com/Draconic-Inc/Draconic-Evolution/blob/1.21/src/main/generated/assets/draconicevolution/lang/en_us.json) identifies fusion energy cost and injector tiers. Reactor and Chaos-island mechanics were not independently traced.

Reference checklist: storage cores, pylons, energy crystals, fusion core, injectors, crafting tiers, modular gear, shields, dislocators, reactor containment, guardian encounters and chaos resources. Untraced entries remain research leads.

Mapping proposal: shared storage/link, condition-driven multiblocks, Modifier and field services, boss content and exotic reactions; M7. High-tier numbers alone do not create a distinct system.

## What we adopt

Visible endgame infrastructure, coordinated high-energy crafting and consequential containment tasks.

## What we reject

Invulnerable gear, unexplained energy inflation and copying “fusion crafting” as a scientific fusion model.

## What we improve

Make late-game value depend on design, information and managed hazards; preserve meaningful material limits and measurable losses. Feed/compiler integration is a proposal requiring conservation and throughput proof.
