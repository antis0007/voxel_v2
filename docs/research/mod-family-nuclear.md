Mapping proposal: neutron/radiation transport, decay bookkeeping, thermal/reaction coupling and beams are new simulation work; fuel recipes and reactor templates are data after that. M4 fission/radiation, M7 fusion/accelerators; cost depends on the plane-budget spike.

## What we adopt

Layout-dependent engineering, coolant management, fuel processing, instrumentation and persistent cleanup work.

## What we reject

Calling simplified game reactions real nuclear predictions, arbitrary free-energy loops and duplicating reactor-specific heat engines.

## What we improve

Tie failure to shared heat, pressure and contamination; give the Primer honest units, named simplifications and observable shielding experiments. Geometry, shielding and criticality remain proposed models needing numerical validation.
