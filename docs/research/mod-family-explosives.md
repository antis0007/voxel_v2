# Explosives, missiles and sensors

Scope: [Addendum A.6](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a6-icbm-classicmodern-defensetech-modular-warfare-style-explosives): ICBM Classic/Modern, DefenseTech, Modular Warfare-style explosives. Claims: [C-MC2/4](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-F1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [ICBM Classic's author repository](https://github.com/BuiltBrokenModding/ICBM-Classic) describes tiered explosives, missiles, launchers and machines; its [changelog](https://github.com/BuiltBrokenModding/ICBM-Classic/blob/prod/1.12/CHANGE_LOG.md) documents radar fixes, chemical blasts and budget concerns for red-matter entities. DefenseTech, Modern and the unnamed Modular Warfare variant were not independently verified.

Mapping proposal: explosion events request pressure, heat, fragments and material products; missiles use BodyController, sensors feed signal networks. Conventional content M4; exotic fields M7. Pressure/fracture and bounded singularities require owned systems, not only data rows.

## What we adopt

Clear warning, targeting, defensive sensing, remote triggering and distinct explosive purposes.

## What we reject

Unbounded terrain scans, radius-only damage that bypasses shared hazards, and treating a “nuclear” game item as a validated reactor model.

## What we improve

Let aftermath persist as collapsed structures, gas, fire and settling dust; disclose detonation energy and containment limits. Conservation, friendly-fire policy and bounded far-field effects are unresolved design obligations.
