# Utility automation and channel routing

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Extra Utilities, Modular Routers, XNet, Laser IO, Cyclic, Industrial Foregoing. Claims: [C-MC1/2/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Modular Routers](https://github.com/desht/ModularRouters) documents a single-slot buffer and installed modules executed in order for movement, inventory pulls and placement; [McJty's XNet docs](https://mcjty.eu/docs/mods/xnet) provide its channel-network reference. [LaserIO](https://github.com/Direwolf20-MC/LaserIO) and [Industrial Foregoing](https://github.com/InnovativeOnlineIndustries/Industrial-Foregoing) repositories establish author sources; their exact machines were not traced. Extra Utilities variants remain unverified; Cyclic source fetch failed.

Mapping proposal: typed network channels, filter predicates, ordered Action-producing modules, shared field/region jobs and ordinary storage. M4; item movement and transaction ownership are shared systems, while machine recipes are data.

## What we adopt

Compact configurable automation, reusable filters and clear direction/order controls.

## What we reject

One custom transport engine per gadget, unbounded remote scans or “void” extraction disconnected from world matter.

## What we improve

Report where items are reserved or blocked and show resource, heat and environmental consequences. Plant gatherers, mob processors, laser drills, black-hole units and exotic utility blocks remain source-specific study leads, not verified details.
