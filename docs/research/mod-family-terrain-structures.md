# Terrain, biomes and generated structures

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): Terralith, Tectonic, Biomes O' Plenty, BYG/Oh The Biomes You'll Go, Dungeon Crawl, Dungeons Enhanced, When Dungeons Arise, Valhelsia Structures, YUNG's, Repurposed Structures. Claims: [C-Z1/2/3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#zork-worldbuilding-not-text-interaction), [C-N3](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#noita-lore-worldbuilding-magic-secrets).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Tectonic's author listing](https://modrinth.com/datapack/tectonic), [Biomes O' Plenty](https://github.com/Glitchfiend/BiomesOPlenty), [Valhelsia Structures](https://github.com/ValhelsiaTeam/Valhelsia-Structures), [YUNG's organization](https://github.com/YUNG-GANG), [Repurposed Structures](https://github.com/TelepathicGrunt/RepurposedStructures) and [When Dungeons Arise's author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/when-dungeons-arise) were reached. [We've Gone](https://github.com/Potion-Studios/Oh-The-Biomes-Weve-Gone) explicitly identifies itself as a **sequel** to BYG; the addendum's BYG and expanded name are one reference, not two mods. Terralith, Dungeon Crawl and Dungeons Enhanced fetches failed; their detailed generation is unverified. No exact structure catalogs were traced.

Mapping proposal: seeded terrain stages, biome/material/resource tables, structure templates and semantic lore tags; M6. Existing [worldgen research](worldgen-noise.md) remains a separate algorithm topic.

## What we adopt

Recognizable terrain silhouettes, biome-local resources and structures with navigation/combat purpose.

## What we reject

Counting renamed references twice, copying authored structures and assuming impressive size establishes coherent culture or good traversal.

## What we improve

Generate physically functional ruins with hints and multiple routes, then connect names/history to their actual mechanisms. Generation budget and density/coherence claims remain unproved.
