# Reference: Zork

Research date: 2026-10-24. Scope: A.1 internally coherent ruins and room specificity, rather than adopting a parser as the main interface. Proposed improvements are unproven.

## Primary-source findings

Original Zork I object definitions include an underground technology school's advertising, zorkmids, a boat company's warranty, temple inscriptions and objects with material/action flags. Their juxtaposition gives institutions and jokes a physical location. [Archived original ZIL source](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1/blob/master/1dungeon.zil)

The original hint text connects the dam's water controls to the Loud Room and explicitly supplies an alternative acoustic solution. This is an important counterexample to the bible's blanket "puzzles with single solutions" criticism. The useful target is systematic multi-solution coverage, not a claim Zork never offered alternatives. [Archived original hint text](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1-gold/blob/master/hint-txt.txt)

These are historical primary artifacts hosted by an archive, not a current publisher endorsement or a reusable-asset license. They establish authored specificity; they do not demonstrate that generated prose or cultures will achieve equivalent quality.

## What we adopt

Give each landmark an operational purpose, connected infrastructure and traces of its users. Names and relic descriptions reference that purpose, economy and institution. Let changing a dam alter downstream water and access. Describe observed materials, light, sound and temperature before uncertain historical interpretation (C-Z1, C-Z2, C-Z4).

## What we reject

Do not gate progress on guessing a unique verb or author-intended item combination. Do not generate cultures as independent lists of names and decorative ruins; architecture, religion-mechanism, economy and downfall must agree. Do not invent sensory facts absent from scene summaries (C-Z1, C-Z3, C-Z4).

## What we improve

Propose a civilization record that supplies naming rules, structures, machines, resources and a simulated downfall trace together (C-Z1; S10 must first establish coherence). Landmark placement records function and traversable distance so the required density can be audited (C-Z2). Each mandatory barrier must expose at least two dialect/physics routes; the bible also requires three successful solution executions, so plans must preserve that stronger evidence requirement and clarify category counting (C-Z3). Text templates consume current plane values and lore provenance, then undergo human comparison with original exemplars (C-Z4). Physical consistency checks and prose review are distinct; neither alone proves Zork-quality writing.
