# Space exploration and life support

Scope: [Addendum A.5](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a5-space-galacticraft-ad-astra-advanced-rocketry-beyond-earth-space-bosstools-extra-planets-mars-base-terrablender-planets--but-better): Galacticraft, Ad Astra, Advanced Rocketry, Beyond Earth, Space BossTools, Extra Planets, Mars Base, Terrablender planets. Claims: [C-F1/2](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#ftl), [C-S5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [Galacticraft's team repository](https://github.com/TeamGalacticraft/Galacticraft) establishes the modern rewrite; [Ad Astra's author repository](https://github.com/terrarium-earth/Ad-Astra) establishes space technology/exploration. Exact life-support and rocket behavior in these versions was not played or source-traced. Other named variants are unverified; “Mars Base” and “Terrablender planets” need an exact author/project identity.

Mapping proposal: worlds carry atmosphere/gravity/thermal constants; gas volumes provide life support, bodies provide vessels, shared controllers provide thrust. An orbital layer and launch/landing bridge are substantial new systems, M6–M7.

## What we adopt

Expeditions, distinctive planetary resources, habitat preparation, rovers and cargo logistics.

## What we reject

Claiming that source mods demonstrate patched-conic orbits, conserved gas mixtures or freely constructed rigid-body rockets.

## What we improve

Make pressure leaks, thermal balance, fuel mass, docking and transfer windows observable engineering problems. Their numerical models and player legibility remain research/spike questions; “reuse existing systems” does not establish feasibility.
