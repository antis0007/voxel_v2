# Recipe discovery, accessories and affixes

Scope: [Addendum A.14](../specs/MOD_ADDENDUM.md#a14-and-more--additional-references-the-matrix-must-include): JEI/EMI, Curios, Apotheosis. Claims: [C-MC3/4/5](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#heavily-modded-minecraft-tech--magic--tinkers), [C-S1](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md#neal-stephenson-snow-crash-the-diamond-age-cryptonomicon-anathem-seveneves-fall-the-baroque-cycles-natural-philosophy).

Primary evidence checked 2026-10-24: [JEI](https://github.com/mezz/JustEnoughItems) and [EMI](https://github.com/emilyploszaj/emi) identify accessible item/recipe viewing. [Curios](https://github.com/TheIllusiveC4/Curios) describes configurable accessory slots and explicitly adds only an inventory GUI by default. [Apotheosis](https://github.com/Shadows-of-Fire/Apotheosis) describes modular enhancements to enchanting and other vanilla systems; exact current affix/boss/reforge rules were not traced.

Mapping proposal: recipe queries/Primer explanations, equipment-slot schemas and the one shared Modifier evaluator for affixes/enchantments; M3 gear/UI and M5 enchantments. Trait resolution is code; generated values follow material tables.

## What we adopt

Searchable “uses/made from” information, configurable equipment slots and readable modifiers with distinct benefits.

## What we reject

Curios as a source of authored items, randomized stat inflation without causes and assuming recipe visibility demonstrates player mastery.

## What we improve

Explain missing prerequisites and material-derived stats in-world, keep secret recipes discoverable through hints, and record demonstrated experiments. Onboarding and new-material coverage remain acceptance targets.
