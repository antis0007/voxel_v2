# Playable tech and goals: ordered slices

Status (2026-10-26): slice 1 spike in progress. Nothing here is accepted yet;
each slice needs its own review capture and gate before it counts as done.

Scope: take the factory and expedition loop from prototype to a complete,
playable tech progression. The reference points are Factorio's automation
pacing, Minecraft's block-first building and Noita's physical matter. This is
a multi-session project. The slices below are ordered by dependency, not by
calendar.

## What exists (survey, 2026-10-26)

- 27 machine kinds in `assets/machines.ron`. Each kind is referenced across
  the factory layer: extraction, induction, multiblock, power, item transit,
  pile, portable and chip logic. A grep for placeholder markers found none.
  Per-kind behaviour has not been audited yet; that audit is the first step of
  slice 3.
- Expedition quests in `world/adventure/quest.rs`: sigils, relics, the workshop
  heart and guardian fights. These are the only explicit goal chain found.
- `world/technology.rs` groups existing recipes into nine capability branches.
  Its header says recipes stay authoritative. It does not gate recipes behind
  research, so tech progression is browsing, not unlocking.
- Visuals lag behind the systems. Machines and pipes were procedural float
  cuboids. The owner rejected that look on 2026-10-25.

## Slice 1: machine casing as authored voxel blocks (spike, in progress)

- Furnace casing is generated by `tools/gen_machine_models.py` into
  `assets/models/machines/furnace.ron`, then loaded through the shared
  vertex-list loader in `mesh/models/wand_asset.rs`.
- Grid: one block is 10 voxels per metre, so each voxel is two texels of the
  20 texel/m world grid.
- Status colour multiplies the ink voxels at draw time. Active furnaces do not
  glow yet, because the shared loader has no per-draw emission.
- Gate: unit test that the asset fills its block; before and after captures
  from `VOXEL_TORCH_REVIEW_MODE=factory`, reviewed by the owner.

## Slice 2: remaining closed casings

Storage, feeder, battery, transformer, fabricator, alloy kiln, bioreactor and
cultivator are closed boxes. One generator family with per-family trims and
glyphs covers them. Gate: same capture recipe, one contact sheet of all nine.

## Slice 3: open-top machines with moving parts

Crusher, washer, mining drill, pump, tank, generator and assembler keep an
open top for machinery. Each needs its own block layout in the generator.
Machinery animation stays on the fixed clock as voxel frames, not float
rotation. Gate: capture of each kind at idle and active.

## Slice 4: conduits on the voxel grid

Cable, item pipe and fluid pipe become square voxel tubes. Junctions and
straight runs are generated per arm combination. Packets stay cubes inside.
Gate: fixture capture with a mixed junction, and a run that crosses a chunk
boundary (negative and large coordinates included, per the world rules).

## Slice 5: emissive status for authored models

Add a per-draw emission channel to the shared loader so lamps and furnace
glyphs glow with machine state. The loader is owned by the wand work in
progress, so this needs coordination before editing it.

## Slice 6: tech progression and goals (owner decision needed)

Open questions for the owner, because each changes what gets built:

- Should recipes or machine kinds unlock through research, or stay available
  from the start? Current code has no gating.
- What is the factory's goal chain? Candidate: power, then smelting, then
  automated mining, then assembly, ending in a multiblock. This needs a
  name-free design, since new proper nouns need approval.
- Any new persistent state needs a save version bump and must follow the
  factory save-format rule.

## Slice 7: content depth and end state

Ore processing chains, chemicals and the induction matrix are listed in
`docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` and the mod-family research notes. They stay out of this
plan until slices 1 to 6 pass review.

## Decisions taken in slice 1 (my call, revisit if wrong)

- Voxel density: 10 per metre, not 8. Eight does not divide the 20 texel/m
  grid; ten gives two texels per voxel.
- Authoring: a Python generator writes the data, mirroring the existing
  `tools/gen_*` scripts. Blender assets in `tools/blender` remain an option for
  detailed pieces.
- Format: reuse the vertex-list loader already in the tree, so machines and
  the wand share one reader.
