# Player progression and the fixed world

2026-10-24. This maps bible A.2/A.5 to system ownership; it is the intended
first-40-hours progression, not a measured playtime result or release schedule.
The [bible](../specs/GAME_BIBLE.md) remains authoritative. Earlier named roles
and depth ranges remain world design constraints, not current generator claims.

## Fixed spine

One planet: green surface over Engineers (~200–600 m), Choristers (~600–1500 m)
and Founders (>1500 m). Generators choose coherent phonotactic names, cultures,
architecture and dialects without changing those roles. Engineer infrastructure
records the mine's cascade failure; resonance temples and shattered ravines
record the Chorister schism; rule-bearing matter and crystalline archives
record the Founders. The sealed deep and non-rock core remain a mystery; this
plan does not choose its nature. The reachable orbital ring preserves records
of the earlier planet. Sky time/moon/seasons are real secret conditions.

Salvagers, Cantors, Archivists, the Crown and the deep cult connect salvage,
trade, taxes, archives and rumor to faction state. Tech and magic are two
notations for the Work; their shared compiler is built early, but revealed
to the player mid-progression. Physical knowledge can outlast characters and
ages only through the specified archive/world rules.

Development order differs from play order: M3 may build the teaching artifact
before the M5 chamber that later appears near the start of a complete game's
journey. This does not redefine the intended player sequence or prove two-hour
automation or forty-hour pacing.

## Modes and knowledge

Persistent survival/creative/education share the same world rules. Delve runs
kill the character while retaining the world, old bodies/bases and codex state;
expeditions connect generated sites with world-state choices. Adventure modules
use structures and the same text/read APIs. Education and onboarding use one
adaptive Primer/codex artifact, with richer grounded room prose in the text
layer; the strict functional-tooltip limits do not erase those room descriptions.

No separate tutorial physics, magic interpreter, orbital inventory economy or
virtual-world engine may bypass its shared owner. Each of the six physical
secret rule families in bible A.2.5 must have generated conditions, an in-world
hint chain and a measurable state change; coordinates alone cannot satisfy it.
