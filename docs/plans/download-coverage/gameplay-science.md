Retain outdoor continuation through valleys, wetlands, quarries, ridges,
forests and roads. Natural habitat and constructed site are separate layers.
World generation seeds a source; later spawn requires birth, reanimation,
manifestation, assembly or migration with actual conditions/inputs/routes.
Darkness alone never materializes creatures in a secured room.

Source -> reserved inputs/conditions -> visible development -> retained ready
output -> emergence. Blocked output stops new work and keeps completed stock.
Transplanting carries stable identity, stage, reservations and ownership;
locational fault taps do not duplicate the underlying flux. Inspections expose
limiting conditions, rates, uncertainty and occupied space. Boss emergence is
deliberate and has enough room. Source/creature/holder/encounter records stay
bounded and invalidate local condition caches by revision.

### Mana, XP, knowledge and automation

All of DL12 E/K2/M2 remains required, even where current habitats implement a
smaller subset:

- Separate common operating mana, spendable XP and retained knowledge. Mana is
  fictional and not labelled joules. XP spending never deletes learned recipes.
- Shared collectors divide one source's flux; storage/conversion/summoning
  cannot multiply an isolated input. Natural and cultivated alternatives may
  remain profitable indefinitely with supplied growth/fuel/flux.
- Player, automated, predator and environmental kills share eligible yields.
  Pay death once per lifecycle/cycle/payout kind. Reanimation/reload does not
  refill spent potential; an actually paid new production/charging cycle may.
  Biological co-products may be separately funded; stored spirit charge stays
  one pool. Cores, corpses, items, eggs and enchantments retain singular stock.
- Preserve peaceful/live/service/kill farms where appropriate, visible source
  capacity, separate XP/mana buffers, real reach/backpressure, persistent
  overflow and production/bottleneck statistics. No hidden anti-farm penalties,
  mandatory escapes, anti-machine immunity or inflation cancelling success.
- Reuse real spell costs/payloads for mounted emitters; provide inspection,
  receivers and local threshold/hysteresis controls before a general editor.
- Working farms' interacting state continues when the player leaves. Stable
  paid processes may bulk-advance only under checked conditions; arbitrary
  combat cannot become estimated kills/minute. No app-closed offline progress
  is authorized. Cohorts must transfer rather than duplicate individuals.

Eight treasure effects stay on the backlog: Winchstep Boots/Anchored Spool,
Kickback Cuff, Bankshot Rune, Surveyor's Edge, Stillstep Fittings, Witness
Lantern, Delay Prism and Catcher's Buckler. The last two share singular stored
spell-payload ownership. Each changes a player action; not every reward must be
a machine component. Full-world rewind, inverted controls, unlimited copying,
universal chemistry/genetics/acoustics/aerodynamics, giant rideable city,
general encounter editor and mandatory remote raids remain D under rev3.
