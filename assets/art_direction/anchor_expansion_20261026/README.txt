Axiom: Anchor art expansion, 26 October 2026

Four original concept sheets, generated using the built-in imagegen tool:
  field_instruments.png  - 12 portable tools with different functional silhouettes
  fabrication.png        - 6 workshop machines and 4 connector-shape callouts
  maintenance_hall.png   - inhabited workshop opening onto the braid freight shaft
  return_cargo.png       - 6 advanced devices and a three-stage reader comparison

Each exact prompt is saved as a neighbouring .prompt.txt file. The existing
equipment_material_study.png was the palette and silhouette reference, followed
by the new sheets as references for one another. These are original design
interpretations, not copied franchise props, Blender renders or game captures.

Palette

anchor32.gpl is an editor-ready GIMP palette; palette.json records the same 32
sRGB colours. Two outline/cavity colours and ten three-tone material ramps cover
walnut, bone, moss, ember, iron, brass, blue, ceramic, silicon and copper.
The generated PNGs are concept references, not quantized 32-colour shipping
atlases. The palette files are the precise authoring target.

Construction progression

Salvaged: repaired timber, wrapped leads, mismatched reused parts, household care.
Anchor: ceramic housings, separate heat paths, signed coils, calibration marks,
keyed sockets, replaceable filters and clearly accessible maintenance panels.
Return: folded ceramic members, dark silicon planes, optical cards and fewer
exposed fasteners. Function persists while the manufacturing process changes.

In all three, identify the job through silhouette and ports before ornament.
Power, signal, fluid and card connections have different shapes. Reserve emission
for working surfaces and indicators. Keep broad material planes quiet; detail
belongs at grips, joints, seals, optics and service access.

Lore grounding

Source: docs/design/WORLD_LORE.md, particularly The office map, Skyhook and
magitech, Future focus: semiconductor tiers, Magic, The Bloom, and Deep time.
manifest.json supplies an era, purpose, physical evidence, motif link and open
question for each of the 24 proposed objects. These manufacturing attributions
are concept proposals, not additions to established world facts. The sender of
the return, the ninth office and the Surveyors remain unresolved.

Inspiration research and creative interpretation

Neal Stephenson's author notes connect Anathem's setting to institutional walls,
a clock and different spans of attention. The design translation here is a
maintained institution whose instruments, records and infrastructure imply time.
https://www.nealstephenson.com/anathem.html

Halo's official character-design interview describes beginning with a gameplay
role, establishing silhouette/proportions in prototypes, then refining. The
translation here is job-first shapes and a clear large-to-small hierarchy, with
original geometry rather than franchise equipment or symbols.
https://www.halowaypoint.com/news/inside-infinite-october-2021

RimWorld's official description joins production, materials, futuristic equipment
and colonists' everyday needs. The translation here is a working bench, repaired
tools, clean access routes and one domestic detail within large infrastructure.
https://rimworldgame.com/

Production boundary

This expansion changes art-reference files only. It adds no engine assets,
recipes, fabrication mechanics, ports, machine simulation or lore revelations.
The object sheets are guides for deliberately drawn icons and appropriately
simple meshes, followed by native gameplay-size review. No tests or hosted CI
were run for these art files. PNG dimensions, palette count, prompt files and
manifest completeness were checked; no performance claim is made.
