ORIGINAL AXIOM CONCEPT ART, not a screenshot or implemented game asset.
STYLE REFERENCE: attached Axiom instrument and fabrication sheets. Continue the same carefully made 16-bit SNES/GBA pixel-art identity, shared dark warm outlines and restrained walnut/bone/moss/ember/iron/brass/blue/ceramic/violet/copper palette. Crisp stepped contours, connected pixel clusters, 3–5 deliberate shades per material, quiet broad planes, sparse highlights at working edges. High fidelity is strong shape and color judgment, not adding micro-detail. Avoid photographic textures, random speckles, smooth vector art, tiny illegible labels, gratuitous rivets, glowing trim everywhere, large neon gems and excessive bloom.
DESIGN: each object has a clearly different functional silhouette, coherent support, visible access, plausible inputs and outputs, and the same shape-coded Power, Signal, Fluid and Card interfaces when needed. The setting joins warm lived-in repair craft and technically sophisticated buried infrastructure. Make original designs, never copies of named Minecraft-mod props. References are conceptual inspiration only.
LORE: the Anchor was a city of engineering offices maintaining the braid freight tether. Crystal cards are stored routines; magic is inherited engineering. The later Ballast adapted machinery into households. The Bloom is an escaped recycling fungus that eats circuit metal. The Keepers' licensed strains can be used safely. Resonant crystal is mined or salvaged: do NOT depict its unsolved manufacture as mastered. Every technology has material and energy costs. Do not reveal the ninth office, depict the Surveyors, or identify who sends returns from above.
FORMAT: attractive readable object study, a uniform charcoal backdrop, generous spacing, small plain labels and discreet footer CONCEPT STUDY. Sparse shared palette strip at bottom; no explanatory paragraphs, fake HUD or brand logos.

SHEET TITLE: ARCHIVE LOGISTICS.
Eight original functional objects in four columns by two rows. This branch translates the repo's Applied Energistics 2, Refined Storage, Storage Drawers, Sophisticated Storage, Integrated Dynamics, ComputerCraft and shared-VM ideas into Axiom's physical Archive. Do not copy mod textures or modern server racks. The governing visual distinction is INFORMATION versus MATERIAL: crystal cards and narrow keyed tabs carry records/signals, while bulk stock stays in containers and moves by a separate cargo route. No infinite matter in a magic cube. Old ledger craft and precise ceramic card sockets belong together.
1 "Stock wall": a compact oak-and-iron six-compartment cabinet; broad window/label on each drawer shows a different clear silhouette of a physical material, a large latch and finite cavity. Quiet beautiful cabinetry, not hundreds of tiny drawers.
2 "Request desk": low pale ceramic and walnut lectern with one vertical card slot, one broad two-line mechanical tally strip, a physical order card clipped to its side, small signal output. No blue holographic monitor.
3 "Pattern press": short wide stone/ceramic working bench with one reusable dark crystal template held above a substrate tray, simple press lever and separate finished-part bin. Reads as manufacture from stock, not magic duplication.
4 "Signal bridge": narrow horizontal dark relay bar with four differently notched plug-in card modules, two deliberate ceramic standoffs and a few visible parallel return paths. Clear paired inlet/outlet topology, sparse blue indicator pixels.
5 "Field crate": portable worn oak chest with iron corners, two broad recessed straps and an OPEN removable interior organizer tray; two small finite item stacks and a repair slot. Related to the established chest, not a military equipment case full of micro-greebles.
6 "Paired couplers": TWO small matching ceramic uprights side by side in the same cell, with corresponding three-notch brass identity tabs and a small removable cost cartridge. Stable paired identity matters. No open fantasy portal, no running stream of free matter.
7 "Archive carrier": low original wheeled utility cart-automaton with two large steady wheels, a small rear support, one visible cargo tray with a single stocked crate, tiny front index sensor. It serves physical logistics rather than combat. Distinct from the garden's four-legged culture courier.
8 "Logic loom": a flat tabletop slate panel with six removable ceramic logic blocks arranged in one legible branching path, shallow brass routing channels and a crystal program card parked at its edge. Designed for a tangible programmable spell/control routine. No illegible code wall, no phone screen, no decorative mandala.
At bottom add a very small explanatory spatial vignette: Stock wall -> short belt and Archive carrier -> dispatch bench.
