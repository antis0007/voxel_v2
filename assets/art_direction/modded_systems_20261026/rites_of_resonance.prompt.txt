SHEET TITLE: RITES OF RESONANCE.
Eight original devices in four columns by two rows. This is the repo's Thaumcraft, Astral Sorcery, Ars Nouveau, Hex Casting and ritual-magic inspiration interpreted THROUGH AXIOM'S LORE. The Orders of Resonance preserve engineering procedures as rites; the Copyists copy their diagrams. Their institutions can look sacred, but the apparatus still has contacts, filters, supports, finite charge, access and working geometry. Do not show generic demon summoning, skull piles, an invented pantheon, copied mod altars or enormous purple flames. Do not imply aspect tags are measured chemistry or invent free power from stars. Make a beautiful mix of ancient scholarly craft and understood/ununderstood precision equipment.
1 "Observation lens": small hand-sized dark iron square sight frame with a pale inset glass plane, one brass adjustment tab and a folded compact stand. Designed to inspect objects, not a fantasy eye icon.
2 "Assay crucible": a supported low ceramic crucible in a copper yoke over a modest heat foot, a separate residue cup and a small reagent ampoule. Material process before magical ornament.
3 "Infusion frame": a broad shallow basalt work platform with three thin supported ceramic posts and a removable crystal-card bridge above a PHYSICALLY SUPPORTED part cradle. Separate material trays flank it. No floating sword, no hovering giant gem.
4 "Resonance cradle": quiet three-point iron-and-ceramic support holding a dark crystal ring at measured contact points, a small tuning lever, narrow coolant return and a dim amber readiness mark. The ring is salvaged, not newly manufactured.
5 "Sky reader": a compact brass/iron armillary-like observational instrument with two broad dark rings, a small optical sight and an engraved physical timetable plate on a pale stone base. It observes the braid/crowns sky, not generic zodiac astrology.
6 "Rite slate": slate tabletop tablet holding four large movable ceramic instruction plates in an obvious ordered path, one fork-shaped routing piece and a crystal card slot. This is a preserved procedure, not illegible magical computer code.
7 "Tithe contact": small insulated wearable cuff laid open on the table, two blunt conductive contact pads, a simple narrow stored-charge index and one release clasp. No person, gore, wound or blood drops. Power is finite and bodily costs must not be glamorized; it resembles a careful measuring/charging interface.
8 "Filter reliquary": a small upright folded ceramic service cabinet, two simple hinged panels opened to reveal a plainly removable pale woven filter, a neatly folded clean cloth and a sealing tab. Its ceremonial resemblance comes from symmetry and careful maintenance, not added crosses or occult symbols.
