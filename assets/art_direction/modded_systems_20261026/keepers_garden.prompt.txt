SHEET TITLE: KEEPERS' SERVICE GARDEN.
Eight objects in four columns by two rows, every object shown in a readable three-quarter isometric view. Explore living magitech and spatial automation using the repo's Botania/Forestry/growth-system inspiration. It must feel like original botany and engineering, not a nursery full of glowing gems or copied mana-flower designs. Generous moss/sage shapes, ivory ceramic and dark iron interfaces; tiny restrained blue/amber accents. Each device shows both an organic part and the simple hardware that bounds or services it.
1 "Fuel bloom": a low ceramic growing bed with broad rust-green leaves, a SMALL side hopper holding clearly finite biomass briquettes, a copper collection contact, and an accessible spent-substrate tray. Beautiful asymmetrical organic silhouette.
2 "Irrigation reed": a graceful narrow bent reed grown around a ceramic fluid elbow; the leaf tip drips into a small stone channel. A visible round inlet and shutoff collar communicate water supply.
3 "Lens pod": two layered dark-green leaves cupping a small FLAT amber optical lens in an iron iris ring, on a short split root stem. No large crystal.
4 "Mycelial filter": removable upright shallow filter cassette, woven pale fibres inside a dark frame, a separate grey spent-board tray, clear sealing lip. Keep dangerous recycling strains enclosed.
5 "Root gate": squat planted wooden-and-ceramic valve frame with a grown root pulling an unmistakable sliding shutter. Clear inlet and outlet and open/closed travel, no sentient tree face.
6 "Seed archive": a tiny tidy oak seed cabinet with six broad labelled compartments, clay seed jars and one pale data card clipped to a drawer. No modern screen.
7 "Culture courier": small friendly utilitarian FOUR-LEGGED ceramic carrier automaton with broad stable feet, a low copper basket holding a sealed culture tray, one moss-colored sensor slit. It carries objects; not a humanoid with decorative wings.
8 "Buffer trough": a shallow contained stone-and-ceramic reservoir with a little level float, separate inlet/outlet collars and one dark resonant card plate in a protected socket. Water/material containment should read; do not show infinite magic liquid or a free energy loop.
