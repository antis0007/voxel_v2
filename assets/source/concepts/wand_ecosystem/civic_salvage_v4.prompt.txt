INITIAL GENERATION
Create ONE original concept sheet of SIX compelling found magical implements for AXIOM, a clean 16-bit modded Minecraft / Noita sandbox. They are salvaged civic tools from a buried orbital-tether anchor, repurposed by people who repair things they no longer know how to make. The previous ceramic-and-brass luxury gadget designs were BORING AND ALGORITHMIC. This is a radical design divergence: six different construction philosophies and proportions, not a matching equipment set. Beautiful restrained pixel art, confident broad color clusters, selective dark outlines, no fine texture noise, crisp enlarged pixels, no shiny 3D renders. Flat desaturated parchment-gray background, 3 columns x2 rows, no borders or text, enough space around every silhouette. Unequal sizes intentional. Each object has ONE memorable gesture. No common fittings, no matching color scheme, no repeated inset-glowing-panel formula, no gemstone on rod, no symmetric ornamental housings. Six standalone hand-held implements, no hands.
TOP LEFT: a tiny crystal-detector wand that looks nearly mundane: a blunt dark wooden splint, a pea-sized dirty plum crystal wedged into a split near one end, a single thin bent silver whisker barely touching it, one faded coral thread tie. Most of its length is bare brown wood. Crooked, humble, ingenious. SMALL and delicate.
TOP MIDDLE: a repurposed elevator brake lever: broad flat blackened steel strip with one strong elbow bend, short ochre-painted grip worn only at the thumb, the distant end broken diagonally exposing a thin pale conductive layer. One missing bolt hole is visible. Looks like a useful old part with strange potential, not a gun. THICK SHORT and offset, almost silhouette-only.
TOP RIGHT: a counterweight sounding tool: dark teardrop-shaped lead plumb bob on a very short pale bone-colored handle attached OFF CENTER, a narrow vertical slit through the weight exposes a little rust-red moving tongue. No glowing lights. An oddly heavy small hand tool, squat suspended mass with negative space. COMPACT WIDE silhouette.
BOTTOM LEFT: a folding archive-card fan: three unequal thin dull-green glass circuit leaves partially folded like a broken pocket rule, held at ONE copper pivot with a short burgundy cloth-wrapped handle, one leaf repaired by a flat ivory patch. Sparse dark etched lines, not ornate gold filigree. FLAT OPEN asymmetrical fan, three large clean planes.
BOTTOM MIDDLE: a long extremely spare ceramic listening hook: chipped warm-white curved strip forms an incomplete lopsided loop at the top, dark graphite inner edge, plain slim gray stem, one small blue repair stitch near the bottom. No crystals and no glowing center. Tall EMPTY SPACE is the identity, like an acoustic measuring tool recovered from the Choir. Slender, quiet, strange.
BOTTOM RIGHT: an old wooden circuit stamp overtaken by the Bloom: squat red-brown rectangular handle, flared flat black stamping foot, two pale yellow fungal brackets replace a missing side of the body, one thin exposed green circuit track under the fungus. Looks useful and slightly unsettling, not a mushroom staff. LOW WIDE blocky silhouette.
Ground every object in physical construction, functional damage and a human repair decision. No random greebles. Avoid covering everything with bands, brackets, chips, tiny wires or gems. 5-7 muted colors per object with a single distinct accent. The entire sheet should communicate Noita-like discoverable oddities with modded Minecraft pixel clarity and memorable silhouettes, not generic fantasy loot. Keep the design tasteful, spare, asymmetrical and visually authored.
