# Environmental authority

`State` owns soil, biological and sediment stocks and pending work. Free water
is encoded by world water levels plus `Fractions`; together they represent one
exact inventory. Chunk-shared immutable fractions also feed rendering.

`geometry` refines heterogeneous edits; `exchange` admits conservative pairs;
`construction` reserves displacement; `cultivation` transfers inventory matter;
`persistence` writes save 31; `inspect` explains actionable conditions.
`activation` selects real fluid/substrate contacts; `sky` caches exact vertical
geometry summaries. Both avoid expanding environmental work through inert air
and bulk water. Receiving-event admission belongs to the actual exchange.

Retain stocks after excavation. Do not change laws with camera distance, render
LOD or quality. Admit all owners before transfers and account for grain volume.
Do not convert a fractional puddle into a full ice inventory item.

See `docs/adr/0019-environmental-ownership.md` for bounds and the unfinished
coupled scheduler, thermal model, trees and full-plan acceptance requirements.
