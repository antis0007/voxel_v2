# Generator 56: settlement construction

The existing settlement footprint and circulation stay in metre space. Houses
gain overhanging roof shells, gable infill, scale-aware timber framing, framed
open windows and masonry bases. Paths use worn earth with gravel shoulders;
the market square retains paving with a border.

All generated parts are ordinary editable blocks. Versions 55 and earlier
retain their existing settlement geometry. No save migration is required.

`place:workshop` and `place:workshop-side` review the settlement at eye height.
The sampler test covers entry clearance and roof occupancy at all three voxel
scales. Native evidence and outstanding art requirements are recorded in
`docs/design/AXIOM_VISUAL_PROGRESS_20261026.md`; build success is not visual
acceptance. Fine-scale windows and roof trim still require native review.
