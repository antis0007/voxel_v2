# Generator 65: the anchor monument

Replaces the first corridor's highway-like composition in new worlds. Generator
64 retains its previous exchange without migration.

The main shaft rises 256 m above the station datum. Six broad curved buttresses
join buried sockets to the shaft; annular collars and an axial anchor-chamber
portal make the load path legible. Dock and control roofs remain under ten metres.
An open arrival axis leads to the shaft instead of crossing beneath bridges.

The outer terrace blends into each terrain column. A long cut-and-fill approach
joins the station datum to natural ground. Foundations, rooms, shaft piers,
buttresses and cover are deterministic editable voxel content. Model cladding
and service fittings add finer material construction. Thin fittings do not
supply collision or power.

The receiver console moves into the low control wing. Workshop and smelter
locations are exposed by the same plan used by sampling and the native review.
Receiver contracts and momentum accounting are unchanged. The parked dock
vessel communicates a service-climber berth; it does not add boarding or a new
automatic transport simulation. Filled crate presentation follows delivered stock.

The atmospheric braid remains the lore's magitech-supported corridor. This does
not turn its 18 km anchorage into an Earth-scale geostationary space elevator.
