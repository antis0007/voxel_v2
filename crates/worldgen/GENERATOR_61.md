# Generator 61: composed smith cottage

The settlement smith cottage uses a cardinal frame centred on voxel boundaries,
paired front and rear window bays, a centred door, complete jambs, aligned heads,
stone sills, timber plates and a clay gable roof. New plaster, dressed limestone
and three grain-oriented sawn oak materials separate built fabric from natural
stone and bark. Existing generator versions retain their previous geometry.

The scope is one benchmark building. Other settlement families are unchanged.
