# Generator 37

New worlds use 37. Saved versions through 36 keep their original generation.

- Building plots survey 81 points across the footprint and apron. Reject steep
  or wet sites; use a median floor instead of the centre column. Small homes
  align their floor to the entrance.
- Plot embankments blend to the local ground at their outer edge. The old
  fixed ramp could end sixteen metres out while still high above the hillside.
- Spawn blends into the surrounding relief over hundreds of metres. A meadow
  beside the western route junction leaves room for early production.
- Cached routes connect actual rotated entrances, the existing descent and
  the meadow. Survey alternative curves, bound the sampled grade to 0.32,
  limit cut/fill, and reject waterways and occupied building footprints.
  Paving and headroom use the same height profile. A return link provides a loop.
- Village placement surveys both riverbanks. Suitable sites get two facing
  rows, a shared graded street and entrance spurs. Unsuitable sites are omitted.
- Coastal falloffs and plate transitions are continuous; plateau slopes and
  deeply cut riverbanks have wider transitions.

Local compilation is verified separately from native traversal, appearance,
generation timing and save compatibility. No tests or gameplay review were
run for this increment. Route bounds describe the sampled plan, not a proven
end-to-end traversal at every seed and voxel scale.
