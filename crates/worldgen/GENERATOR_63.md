through the normal interaction prompt. The cradle is in the pool, where real
water contact enables the existing Wet/frost/shock reactions. No water is added
or removed by the encounter. Earlier generator versions keep their content.

bitset. It can be learned before awakening the guardian. The lens and archive
retain their water-based access rule; fighting is not a new required route flag.

normal encounter-grid range of supported saved actor positions. The encounter
