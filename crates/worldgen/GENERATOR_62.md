Generator 62 adds surveyed river bridges with graded approaches, timber decks,
stone curbs and abutments, railings, beams, and paired piers. Water remains open
under the spans. Routes reject excessive grades and unsupported long crossings.
A bounded search supplies a nearby crossing when the settlement network has none.

Overlapping river/lake surfaces use the lower connected level, and overlapping
regional wet channels merge their surface levels. This prevents a higher water
source from creating a vertical curtain over the lower channel.

All changes are gated at 62. Existing worlds keep their stored generator version.
Native review camera: VOXEL_REVIEW_DUNGEON=place:bridge.
Enable it with a static `--benchmark-terrain-camera=0,0,10,0,0` benchmark.
