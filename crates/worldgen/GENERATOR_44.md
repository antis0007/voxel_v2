# Generator 44: inhabited routes and excavation spaces

This architecture pass starts at 44. All additions are gated at 44; earlier generation paths,
saved edits and the world storage format remain unchanged.

- Castle courts distinguish the central gate route and transverse threshold
  lanes from weathered paving. Existing building geometry retains precedence.
- Market settlements clear a front entrance aisle through legacy furniture and
  partitions, add gardens between plots and seats beside the square. Main and
  rear streets retain their existing footprint and grade.
- Valley towers gain a broad entrance landing with paired illuminated posts.
- Mine galleries widen into copper and tin working faces, with lights beside
  existing supports. The central decline and its grade are retained.
- Pit landing rooms gain perimeter courses, side work bays and lighting, while
  retaining the east landing-to-cache aisle.
- Dungeon floor borders follow shaped room outlines; corridor edge courses
  continue over ramps without changing floor heights.

This is a bounded architecture pass. It does not introduce a city district
planner or certify collision/traversal across every seed and voxel scale.
Native visual evidence and validation are recorded separately from source work.
