# Generator 64: Meridian ground exchange

Adds one abandoned, reclaimable exchange at the first braid corridor, (1100,
-900). The terrain-only floor datum is cached per configuration. Bounds reject
before terrain lookup; all sampling is deterministic and scale-aware.

The court, stairs, walls, galleries, broken portal beams, supports, workshop
blocks, rubble and supported torch lights are actual editable voxel content.
Damage leaves the central cargo route and surviving west gallery intact. Thin
railings, pressure-vessel housings and service frames are app scene fittings;
they do not supply collision, power or automatic cargo movement.

The receiver's existing Deploy action can reclaim the surviving console for its
normal materials cost. It preserves the finite receiver and momentum ledgers.
The smelter and workshop use existing interactions. No new factory network is
implied by decorative copper pipes. Only the first corridor receives this base.

Earlier generators retain their terrain. Existing saves are not upgraded.
Superflat remains unchanged. The orbital rendering pass applies independently
of the generator. Version 63 belongs to separate concurrent world work.
