# Generator 39: terrain, architecture and placement

New worlds use 39. Earlier generator versions retain their generation paths;
existing worlds are not migrated or regenerated by this change.

## Terrain

- A shared metre-space drainage field adds broad valley shoulders, narrow
  ravines/gulches, mountain spurs and varied crests. Resident and horizon
  height queries use the same field, before the existing authored landforms.
- Jittered cells place flared descending cave mouths and sinkhole bowls with
  underground chambers. Horizontal opening parameters are cached per column;
  they do not introduce neighbouring terrain queries per underground voxel.
- Spawn and low coastal ground remain protected. Opening candidates are
  accepted by their centre so the spawn exclusion cannot bisect a tunnel.

## Placement and architecture

- Plot surveys use at most 3 m horizontal spacing and reject wet ground,
  excessive relief, deliberate cave mouths and authored reservations.
- Ruins and their attached boss courts share a surveyed elevation. Courts and
  small homes gain graded aprons and foundations. Home jitter leaves room for
  the apron inside its owner cell; reservations include attached boss courts.
- Rotated surface sites use candidate-owner lookup instead of the old fixed
  48 m X cutoff, which could truncate mines and castle aprons.
- Random dungeon plans survey complete room/corridor roofs, then lower the
  whole connected graph together for six metres of sampled cover. Floors,
  ramps and encounter anchors retain their relative heights. Reject layouts
  that would hit bedrock, require over 48 m of lowering, or overlap authored
  terrain reservations. These surveys are cached with the plan.
- Version 39 dungeon rooms are no longer clipped voxel-by-voxel against the
  surface. Gatehouses have surveyed aprons. Their shafts use annular helical
  treads and a central pier instead of disconnected floating footholds.
- Common buildings receive bay framing, masonry courses, buttresses,
  projecting sills/hoods and cornices. Shared ground-floor door openings take
  precedence over intersecting curtain/wing walls. Castle keeps and selected
  watch turrets rise above the remaining towers; resized wings rebuild their
  interior stairs and room plans. Roof footprints clear vegetation overhead.

## Enemy presentation (all worlds)

Warden, porter, skitterer, kiln, crawler and heavy fallback movement now uses
separate stance and swing phases driven by distance travelled. Feet lift only
on swing. Heavy fallback legs articulate at the knee. Warden/heavy weapon
release has a fast strike and slower follow-through. Common bodies receive
subtle idle breathing, weight shift and recoil; health/tell markers remain
outside body transforms. Collision, damage, attack timing and boss-specific
animation systems are unchanged.

## Evidence and limits

Local offline `cargo check` passed for worldgen and the application; the app
build script also validated its 18 composed WGSL programs. No tests, CI,
gameplay launch, screenshot review, performance benchmark or save-compatibility
run was performed. This is source/compilation evidence, not visual acceptance.
Terrain cover and relief are sampled constraints, not a proof for every seed
and scale. Site density, cave connectivity, shaft traversal, near/far silhouette
agreement and the final Minecraft/Noita visual direction need native review.
